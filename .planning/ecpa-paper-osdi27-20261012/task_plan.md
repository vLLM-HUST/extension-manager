# ECPA OSDI '27 paper plan

## Objective

Prepare a defensible OSDI '27 Research-track submission without promoting
release qualification, fixtures, modeled outcomes, or successful import into
formal runtime-effect or performance evidence.

## Milestones

1. Freeze the venue contract, deadlines, anonymity constraints, and research
   versus operational-track decision.
2. Admit and execute the preregistered matched formal-real experiment.
3. Complete real multi-process effect, failure, recovery, rollback, cleanup,
   and overhead measurements with raw provenance.
4. Rewrite the abstract, introduction, evaluation, and limitations around
   observed results; engage systems and ML-serving baselines.
5. Fit the self-contained body into 12 pages, build cleanly, and create a
   private anonymous submission rendering.
6. Register a substantive abstract by 2026-12-01 22:59 UTC and submit by
   2026-12-08 22:59 UTC only if the evidence gates pass.

## Stop-loss

Do not submit a claims-inflated paper merely to meet the deadline. If the
formal-real and overhead gates remain incomplete, preserve the artifact and
paper for the next appropriate systems deadline.
